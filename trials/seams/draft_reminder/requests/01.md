# Reminders under 160 characters are billed as two or three messages

Our SMS bill has gone up by about a third, although every reminder is within the 160-character limit.

The SMS provider explained it: a text message holds 160 characters only if every character belongs to the basic SMS alphabet. One character outside it (a smiley, a curly apostrophe, a long dash) and the whole message is sent in another encoding where each part holds 70 characters. So a 150-character reminder with one smiley in it is sent, and billed, as three messages. On some older phones it also arrives in pieces, out of order.

Example of a reminder that went out last week, 94 characters, billed as two messages:

> Hi Marta — don’t forget your appointment at Riverside Clinic on 4 Oct at 09:30 😊 See you then!

What I want: every reminder that goes out is a single message in the basic SMS alphabet. I don't want to lose the friendly reminders over a detail: a curly apostrophe or a long dash should simply become its plain equivalent, and a smiley can go. Our patients include people named Zoë and José, and our provider tells me those letters are in the basic alphabet, so their names must stay as they are.
