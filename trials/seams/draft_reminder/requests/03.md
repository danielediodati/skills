# A reminder told a patient to call a number that isn't ours

Last week a patient showed the receptionist this reminder:

> Hi Tom, we look forward to seeing you at Riverside Clinic on 12 Oct at 14:00. Need to reschedule? Call 020 7946 0999.

Our number is 020 7946 0131. Nobody here knows whose number the other one is. The patient had tried to call it to move the appointment, got no answer and came in anyway, annoyed. I then went through the reminders sent this month and found two more with a phone number that isn't the clinic's, and one with ours but two digits swapped.

For a clinic this is serious: a patient could end up giving personal details to a stranger.

What I want: no reminder goes out with a phone number other than the clinic's own, the one on the appointment. The same number written with different spacing or punctuation (`02079460131`, `020-7946-0131`) is fine. A reminder with no phone number in it at all is fine too. The date and the time of the appointment must of course not be mistaken for phone numbers.
