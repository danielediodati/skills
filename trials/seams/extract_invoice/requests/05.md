# Correct invoices with VAT are sent to a clerk as if they didn't add up

I supervise the clerks who review the invoices the tool sets aside. The review pile is supposed to hold the invoices whose figures don't add up. In practice most of what is in it adds up perfectly.

Take invoice BP-2291 from Brightwell Paper Ltd: 12 boxes of A4 paper at 24.50 and one delivery charge of 15.00, which makes 309.00. Below that the invoice shows VAT at 20%, 61.80, and an amount due of 370.80. Every figure on the page is right. The tool reported the lines as coming to 309.00, the total as 370.80, and marked the invoice as a mismatch, so it went to a clerk instead of the ledger.

Nearly all our suppliers charge VAT, so this is the normal case, not the exception. My clerks spend their mornings opening invoices, redoing the sum on a calculator, finding nothing wrong and passing them through. The few invoices with a real error, a wrong quantity or a total that was mistyped, are buried among them and get the same tired look as the rest.

I want an invoice whose own figures are consistent to go straight to the ledger, and the review pile to contain only invoices where something really doesn't add up. The Brightwell invoice should have gone through without anyone touching it.
