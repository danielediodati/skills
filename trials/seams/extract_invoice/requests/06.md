# An invoice with a delivery charge or a flat fee is rejected outright

I work in accounts payable. A lot of our invoices fail to load because of one ordinary line: a charge that is a single amount, with no quantity and no price per unit next to it.

A typical case is invoice NF-0718 from Northgate Fabrication Ltd, dated 21 September 2026. It reads:

> 40 steel brackets at 6.25 — 250.00
> Delivery — 35.00
> Amount due — 285.00

Nothing is missing from that invoice and the figures add up. Instead of a record I got the message `line 2 quantity is missing`. The same week an invoice from our IT contractor failed the same way over a line that said "Annual support fee — 1,200.00", and one from a printer over "Artwork setup — 60.00".

Delivery, setup fees, call-out charges, annual subscriptions: suppliers print these as one amount, and nobody would think to write "1 × 35.00" for a delivery. Yet each of these invoices is refused completely, so a clerk types the whole thing into the ledger by hand, including the lines that were perfectly readable.

I want an invoice like this to load like any other. The Northgate invoice should have come through with its two lines, the delivery charge at 35.00, and a total of 285.00 that matches the lines, without anyone retyping it.
