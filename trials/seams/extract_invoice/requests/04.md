# Invoices from our German and Italian suppliers are rejected over the way they write amounts

I work in accounts payable. About a third of our suppliers are on the continent, and their invoices fail to load far more often than the British ones.

A typical case: invoice KW-10482 from Kessler Werkzeug GmbH, dated 14 September 2026, three lines, amount due printed as "1.250,00 EUR". Instead of a record I got the message `total is not a number: '1.250,00'`. The same day an invoice from Cartiera Lombarda failed with `line 2 unit_price is not a number: '2.340,50'`. Both invoices are perfectly clear to a person: one thousand two hundred and fifty euros, and two thousand three hundred and forty euros fifty.

These invoices don't even reach the clerk's review pile, where at least the figures would already be filled in. They are refused outright, so someone has to type the whole invoice into the ledger by hand, which is the work this tool was meant to take away. It happens mostly on the larger invoices, the ones where a typing mistake costs us the most.

I'd like an invoice whose amounts are clearly readable on the page to be loaded with the right figures, however the supplier's country writes its numbers. The Kessler invoice should have come through with a total of 1250.00.
