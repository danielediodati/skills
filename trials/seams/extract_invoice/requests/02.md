# Every invoice with VAT is flagged as a mismatch

Almost all our invoices carry VAT, and almost all of them come back as `mismatch`, so the clerks check nearly everything by hand and the flag has stopped meaning anything.

Example, an invoice from our stationery supplier:

```
12 x A4 paper, box      24.50      294.00
 1 x Delivery           15.00       15.00
Net                                309.00
VAT 22%                             67.98
Total due                          376.98
```

It comes back with a lines total of 309.00, a total of 376.98 and status `mismatch`. The invoice is perfectly correct: the lines are net and the amount due includes the tax.

What I want:

- the tax on an invoice is recognised and reported in the result as its own amount, next to the lines total and the total;
- an invoice is `ok` when the lines plus the tax give the total, and `mismatch` only when they don't;
- invoices with no tax on them keep working as they do today.
