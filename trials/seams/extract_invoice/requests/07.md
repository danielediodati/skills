# Invoices go into the ledger with the day and month of the issue date swapped

I run the payment schedule for accounts payable. Every so often an invoice reaches the ledger with an issue date that is months away from the one printed on it, and nothing marks it as doubtful.

Invoice BP-2304 from Brightwell Paper Ltd is the one that caught us. Brightwell is a British supplier and the invoice is dated "05/08/2026", meaning 5 August. It was loaded with an issue date of 8 May 2026. Every other field was right, the figures added up, and it went straight into the ledger.

Our terms with Brightwell are 30 days from the issue date. With a date in May, the invoice showed up as two months overdue on the day it arrived. It jumped to the top of the next payment run and was paid three weeks earlier than it needed to be, and it sat in our overdue report for the month, which I then had to explain to the finance director.

We have seen it go the other way too. An invoice from an American supplier dated "03/04/2026", meaning 4 March, was loaded as 3 April, so it looked a month younger than it was and we paid it late.

The date is the one field my clerks don't recheck, because nothing ever flags it. I want the issue date in the ledger to be the day the supplier actually issued the invoice: the Brightwell invoice should have gone in as 5 August 2026. What I can't have is a guessed date going into the ledger looking just as certain as all the others.
