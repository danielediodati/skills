# Long tickets get routed on their opening lines, not on what the customer is asking

I run the support desk. Tickets that contain a long message are regularly sorted as if only the beginning had been read.

Last week a customer on the Pro plan replied to an old thread about a delivery address. Their email client quoted the whole earlier exchange first, about three pages of it, and their new message came after: their card had been charged 1,480 EUR instead of 148 EUR and they wanted it reversed before their rent went out. The ticket appeared in the shipping queue at low urgency, with the summary "Customer wants to update the delivery address". It sat there for two days before a shipping agent opened it and passed it on to billing.

We see the same thing with customers who paste a long error log and put their actual question underneath it, and with people who tell the whole story in order and only say what they need in the last paragraph.

For the customer this means a slow answer on exactly the tickets they put the most effort into. For us it means agents in the wrong queue opening tickets they can't handle.

What I want is for a long ticket to be routed and summarised on what the customer is actually asking for, wherever in the message they say it. The ticket above should have reached billing, with a summary about the overcharge.
