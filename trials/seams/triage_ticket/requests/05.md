# I can't tell which tickets in General were never really sorted

I lead the team that works the general queue. Most of what lands there belongs there: questions about opening hours, feedback, things that fit nowhere else. But a few times a week a ticket turns up in General that plainly isn't general, and nothing in the ticket list sets it apart from the rest.

An example from last Tuesday. Subject: "Help!!". Body: a warehouse manager telling us that none of their stations had been able to print shipping labels since 7 am and that forty orders were waiting on the dock. In our list it showed as general queue, normal urgency, answer within 24 hours, and the summary was just "Help!!" again. It looked like every other low-stakes ticket around it, so nobody opened it until the next morning. The customer had lost a full day of dispatches by then.

When I look back at these tickets they all have the same look: General, normal, and a summary that only repeats the subject line. It gives the impression that the ticket wasn't actually read by anything before it reached us. But a real general ticket with a short subject can look exactly the same, so my agents have no way of knowing which ones to distrust.

I'd like to be able to see, from the ticket list, which tickets were genuinely judged to be general and of normal urgency and which ones arrived without a real verdict, so that a person looks at the second kind straight away rather than a day later.
