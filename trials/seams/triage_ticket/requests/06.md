# Card numbers and passwords from the customer's message end up in the ticket list

I'm responsible for data protection on the support desk. The one-line summary that appears next to each ticket in the agents' list sometimes repeats private details the customer typed into their message.

Two from this month. A customer wrote in about a double charge and, trying to be helpful, typed out their full card number and security code. The summary in the list read:

> Wants the duplicate charge on card 4539 1488 0343 6467 (CVC 318) reversed.

Another customer who couldn't sign in included the password they had been trying. The summary read "Can't log in with password Tulip-Harbour-92 and wants access restored".

Customers will always overshare, and inside the ticket that is manageable: only the agent who opens it sees the message, and we can redact it there. The ticket list is different. Every agent in every queue scrolls through it all day, it is up on the wall screen in the support room, and the summaries go into the weekly report that is emailed to managers outside the team. A card number in a summary has been seen by forty people and copied into a spreadsheet before anyone handles the ticket.

I want the summary to tell an agent what the customer needs without showing details that only the person handling the ticket should see. The first ticket above would have been just as easy to route with "Wants a duplicate card charge reversed".
