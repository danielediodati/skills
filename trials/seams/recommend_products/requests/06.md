# Some recommended products appear with nothing written under them

I look after the look of the shop's results page. In the "Recommended for your search" strip, each product is meant to have a short line underneath saying why it suits the search. Some products turn up with that line empty.

Yesterday a search for "wireless headphones" gave a strip of three. The Aria wireless headphones had "Cable-free, with the best sound in our range" under them. Next to them, the Pico wired earbuds and the 2 m USB-C cable had nothing: name, price, rating, and a blank gap where the reason goes.

The reason is the whole point of the strip. Without it, the strip is just a second, shorter list of results. And these were exactly the two products that needed explaining: a shopper who searched for wireless headphones and is shown wired earbuds with no word of why will conclude the shop didn't understand them.

It also looks broken. The cards in the strip are the same height, so a card with no reason has a hole in it, and when two out of three are like that the strip looks like it failed to finish loading.

I want every product that appears in the strip to come with a reason a shopper can read. In the search above, I should have seen either why the earbuds and the cable were worth my attention, or a strip that didn't leave me wondering.
