# The strip recommends unrelated products for things we don't sell

I manage the catalogue for the shop. We sell audio gear and cables. When a shopper searches for something we don't stock, the "Recommended for your search" strip still fills up, with products that have nothing to do with what they asked for.

Last week someone searched for "kettle". We have never sold a kettle. The strip at the top of the page showed the Aria wireless headphones with the reason "A popular choice with our customers", the 2 m USB-C cable with "A highly rated everyday essential", and the Pico wired earbuds with "Great value at a low price". Below the strip, the page correctly said there were no results for "kettle".

So the first thing that shopper saw, under a heading that says "Recommended for your search", was three things that are not kettles, each with a cheerful reason that doesn't mention their search at all. It reads as if the shop either didn't understand the word or is pushing whatever it has. We get the same effect on misspellings and on searches for brands we don't carry.

It also undermines the strip on the searches where it works. A shopper who has once seen headphones recommended for "kettle" has no reason to believe the strip the next time it shows them headphones.

I want the strip to show only products that genuinely suit what the shopper searched for. For "kettle", nothing we stock does, and the page should not pretend otherwise.
