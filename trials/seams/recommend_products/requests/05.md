# Reasons under recommended products stop in the middle of a word

I look after the look of the shop's results page. The short reason shown under each product in the "Recommended for your search" strip is often cut off partway through, sometimes in the middle of a word.

From a search for "headphones for travel" yesterday, under the Aria wireless headphones:

> Blocks out engine noise on long flights and the battery easily lasts a whole wor

And under the Pico wired earbuds, in the same strip:

> Small enough for a jacket pocket and needs no charging, so a good backup if you

The strip sits at the very top of the page and is the first thing a shopper reads. A sentence that just stops looks like a broken page, and it makes the recommendation itself look careless, which is the opposite of what the strip is for. In the second example the cut even drops the point the sentence was building up to.

I'm happy with the amount of space the reason takes up; I don't want it to grow. What I want is for every reason shown in the strip to read as a complete thought that fits in that space.
