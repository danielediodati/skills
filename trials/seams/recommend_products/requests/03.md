# The reason under a product sometimes quotes the wrong price

The short reason shown under each recommended product sometimes mentions a price, and it isn't always the price of the product.

Example from Monday: the Pico wired earbuds, which cost 19.90, appeared with the reason "A bargain at only €14.90". A customer ordered them, saw 19.90 at checkout and wrote to us asking for the advertised price. Legally we are on thin ice when that happens. Another one, on a 9.50 cable: "Half the price of the 2 m version, just $4".

A reason that quotes the correct price is fine ("Solid choice at 19.90"), and so are reasons with other numbers in them ("2 m long", "30-hour battery").

What I want: a recommendation never goes out with a reason that states a price different from the product's actual price. When that happens, show the product without a reason rather than drop the product from the strip.
