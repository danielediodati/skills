# Action items go missing after a long meeting, and nothing says so

I organise our quarterly planning day. It is a long meeting and it ends with a lot of follow-ups. The list the tool gave me afterwards looked complete, and it wasn't.

Going through my notes later I counted fourteen things people had agreed to do. The list had ten. The four that were missing were all tasks nobody had put a date on, among them "Renew the venue contract" (Tom Becker) and "Ask legal about the new supplier terms" (Priya Raman). There was no sign anywhere that the list had been shortened: no count, no note, nothing. It read like the full set, so I sent it round as the full set.

Three weeks on, the venue contract had not been renewed, because it was on nobody's list. Tom remembered agreeing to it only when I showed him the notes.

The short meetings are fine. It is exactly the big ones, where I most need the list because I can't hold it all in my head, that lose items. And the ones lost are the undated ones, which already tend to slip.

I want to be able to trust that the list I send round covers everything that was agreed. After the planning day I should have ended up with all fourteen follow-ups in front of me, or at the very least have known that I didn't.
