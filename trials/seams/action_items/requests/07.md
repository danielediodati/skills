# A task given to someone who wasn't in the meeting looks like a task nobody took

I run our project meetings and send the follow-up list round afterwards. Quite often a follow-up belongs to someone who wasn't in the room: a colleague who was off that day, or someone in another department. On the list, those tasks appear with nobody's name on them.

From the meeting of 3 March, with Ana Ruiz, Tom Becker and Priya Raman present, my notes said:

> Marta Olsen from finance couldn't join. Ana spoke to her this morning: Marta will approve the budget by 10 March.

The list showed "Approve the budget", due 10 March, with no owner. Marta's name was nowhere on it.

Anyone reading that line takes it for a task that was left hanging. Tom did: he read the list, saw an unowned budget approval with a deadline a week away, and spent an afternoon chasing finance for something Marta already had in hand. Meanwhile Marta, who is exactly the person who needs to see the list because she missed the meeting, was not on it and got nothing from me.

Tasks that really were left without an owner exist too, and I need to chase those. As it is I can't tell the two kinds apart without rereading the notes.

I want the list to tell me whose task it is whenever the notes say so, including people who weren't there. For that meeting, the budget approval should have shown up as Marta Olsen's, and I should have been able to see that it was hers and not an open question.
