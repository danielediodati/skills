# Deadlines we agreed as "by Friday" come out on the wrong day, or with no date

I take the notes for our weekly project meeting and send the follow-up list round afterwards. Deadlines I wrote down as a calendar date come out right. Deadlines I wrote down the way people actually say them in a meeting often don't.

Our meeting of Tuesday 3 March had Ana Ruiz, Tom Becker and Priya Raman in it. My notes said:

> Ana will send the revised quote to Halden by Friday. Tom confirms the venue by the end of next week. Priya orders the badges by 10 March.

The list had all three tasks with the right owners. Priya's badges were due 10 March, as agreed. Ana's quote was due 5 March, which is the Thursday, a day earlier than she promised. Tom's venue had no deadline at all.

Both mistakes cost us something. Ana got a reminder a day early for something she had planned for Friday, and was rightly annoyed. Tom's task sank to the bottom of the list with the undated ones, where it looks like something to get round to, and the venue was still unconfirmed two weeks later. And because some dates are right, I can't tell which ones to check without going back through the notes line by line.

I want the deadlines on the list to be the ones we agreed in the room. For that meeting, the quote should have been due on Friday 6 March and the venue on Friday 13 March.
