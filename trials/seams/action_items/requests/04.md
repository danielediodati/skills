# Tasks come out with no owner although the notes say who took them

I take the notes for our weekly project meeting and send the follow-up list round afterwards. A good share of the items arrive with nobody assigned, even though the notes name the person in plain words.

From the meeting of 3 March, with Ana Ruiz, Tom Becker and Priya Raman present, my notes said:

> Ana will send the revised quote to Halden by the 6th. Tom looks into the venue. Priya to order the badges.

The list I got back had all three tasks, with the right date on the first, but "Look into the venue" and "Order the badges" had no owner. Nobody else in the meeting is called Tom or Priya. I write notes the way everyone does, with first names, and sometimes with the name people actually go by ("Tommy", "Ana R.") rather than the full name in the invitation.

An item without an owner is the one that doesn't get done. So before I send the list I go back through the notes and fill the names in by hand, which is the rereading the tool was supposed to spare me. When I load the list into our task tracker instead, the unowned tasks land in a shared backlog that nobody watches.

I want a task to come out with its owner whenever the notes make clear which of the people in the meeting took it. In the example above, the venue should belong to Tom Becker and the badges to Priya Raman.
