# E2 - the line a replay is judged on

Seams: planner (`src/trainer/coach/planner.py`, `src/trainer/plan.py`, and any document its prompts read)

Where the design puts it: TRAINER.md, "constrain the form, never the content"; the agent chooses the tools, the exercises, their order and the rest.

## Free
- How many exercises and sets each muscle group gets, and how a session is shaped: the planner chooses, per request, from how the user wants to train and the time given. A methodology may give it principles, and ranges to choose inside; counts of exercises or sets per group or per session, a fixed shape of session or a fixed order take the choice over.
